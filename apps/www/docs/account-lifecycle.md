# DLavie Account Lifecycle

## Purpose of DLavie Account

DLavie Account is the connected identity layer for DLavieOS, DLavie AI, DLavie Commerce/PPOB, E-Commerce, Automation, and future DLavie services.

## Purpose of DLavie Card

DLavie Card is the account identity card that communicates user status, tier, validity, and access readiness across the DLavie ecosystem.

## Starter validity

New accounts receive 30 days of starter DLavie Card validity.

## Renewal plans

- Rp5.000 / 30 days
- Rp25.000 / 1 year
- Rp50.000 / lifetime/permanent identity

## Recommended lifecycle

- active
- expired
- grace
- suspended
- archived

## Important policy

Blacklist is only for abuse, fraud, spam, or policy violation. Ordinary DLavie Card expiry should not be treated as blacklist status.
